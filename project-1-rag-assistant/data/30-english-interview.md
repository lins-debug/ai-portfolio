# 英文面试准备

## 自我介绍

模板：我叫 XX，有 X 年 XX 经验。擅长 XX 技术。最近在做一个 XX 项目，用了 XX 和 XX，实现了 XX 效果。对贵公司的 XX 方向很感兴趣。

## 常见问题

Tell me about yourself. Why do you want to work here? What is your biggest weakness? Describe a challenge you faced and how you solved it. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

## 技术面试英文

Explain the architecture of your project. Walk me through the RAG pipeline. Why did you choose Chroma over other vector databases? How do you handle when the system retrieves irrelevant documents?

## 沟通技巧

不要追求完美语法，清晰表达思路比措辞重要。遇到不会的问题诚实说不太确定，但展示思考过程。准备好一两个"反问面试官"的问题，体现你的兴趣和思考深度。
